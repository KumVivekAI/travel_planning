# Visit Singapore: Essential Travel Information

**Source:** Visit Singapore — Essential Travel Information  
**URL:** https://www.visitsingapore.com/travel-tips-travelling-to-singapore/about-singapore/

## Climate

Singapore has a tropical rainforest climate. Temperatures typically range from 25°C to 32°C (77°F to 90°F) year-round. Humidity is high, often above 80%.

### Rainfall Patterns
- Wettest months: November, December, and January (northeast monsoon)
- Slightly drier months: February to April
- Afternoon thunderstorms are common; rain showers are usually brief but intense
- Carry a compact umbrella or light rain jacket

### Packing Tips
- Light, breathable clothing
- Comfortable walking shoes
- Sun protection (hat, sunscreen)
- Light cardigan for air-conditioned malls and museums

## Language and Communication

English is the main language of business and tourism. Most signs are in English. Staff at hotels, attractions, and transport hubs generally speak English. Free Wi-Fi is available at many public areas, malls, and the airport.

## Currency

The Singapore Dollar (SGD) is the local currency. Credit cards are widely accepted. ATMs are available across the island. Some hawker stalls prefer cash.

## Getting Around

Changi Airport is consistently rated among the world's best airports. The Airport MRT connects to the city in about 30 minutes.

The public transport system is integrated:
- MRT trains run from early morning until around midnight
- Buses cover areas not served by MRT
- Tourist passes (1/2/3-day) offer unlimited travel on basic bus and train services

## Family Travel

Singapore is very family-friendly:
- Stroller-accessible paths in major attractions
- Nursing rooms in malls
- Kid-friendly museums and parks
- Universal Studios, Zoo, and Science Centre appeal to children

### Family Activity Suggestions
- Singapore Zoo and River Wonders
- Science Centre Singapore
- Gardens by the Bay Children's Garden
- Beach time at Sentosa or East Coast Park
- Duck tours and river cruises

## Safety and Health

Singapore is one of the safest cities globally. Tap water is safe to drink. Mosquito repellent is advisable for outdoor parks. Travel insurance is recommended.

## Useful Services

- Tourist Information Centres at Orchard Road and Changi Airport
- SG Arrival Card for immigration (check latest requirements before travel)
- 24-hour pharmacies and clinics in major districts

## Connectivity

Prepaid SIM cards and eSIM options are available at the airport and convenience stores. Major carriers include Singtel, StarHub, and M1.
