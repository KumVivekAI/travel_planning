# Visit Singapore: Things to Do

**Source:** Visit Singapore — Things to Do  
**URL:** https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/desktop/en/things-to-do.html

## Attractions by Interest

### Iconic Landmarks
Marina Bay Sands, Merlion Park, Gardens by the Bay, Jewel Changi Rain Vortex.

### Culture and Heritage
Chinatown, Little India, Kampong Glam, National Museum, Asian Civilisations Museum.

### Nature
Singapore Botanic Gardens, MacRitchie Reservoir, Southern Ridges, Pulau Ubin.

### Family Activities
Singapore Zoo, River Wonders, Night Safari, S.E.A. Aquarium, Universal Studios Singapore, Science Centre.

### Arts and Entertainment
National Gallery, ArtScience Museum, Esplanade performances, Marina Bay light shows.

## Indoor Activities
National Gallery, ArtScience Museum, S.E.A. Aquarium, Cloud Forest dome, Trick Eye Museum, indoor malls along Orchard Road.

## Outdoor Activities
Supertree Grove, East Coast Park, Sentosa beaches, Southern Ridges hike, Botanic Gardens walks.

## Neighbourhood Experiences
- **Chinatown:** temples, hawker food, heritage shophouses
- **Little India:** Tekka Centre, spice shops, Sri Veeramakaliamman Temple
- **Kampong Glam:** Sultan Mosque, Haji Lane boutiques
- **Joo Chiat:** Peranakan shophouses and laksa

## Practical Tips
Book popular attractions in advance on peak weekends. MRT is the easiest way between districts. Carry water and sun protection for outdoor plans.
