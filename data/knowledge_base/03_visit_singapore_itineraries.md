# Visit Singapore: Sample Itineraries and Things to Do

**Source:** Visit Singapore — Sample Itineraries & Things to Do  
**URL:** https://www.visitsingapore.com/content/desktop/en/things-to-do/itineraries.html

## One-Day Highlights

Morning: Merlion Park and Marina Bay Sands SkyPark (observation deck). Midday: Gardens by the Bay (Flower Dome and Cloud Forest). Afternoon: Chinatown exploration. Evening: Clarke Quay dinner and Singapore River cruise.

## Two-Day Itinerary

**Day 1:** Marina Bay (Merlion, Gardens by the Bay, ArtScience Museum). Evening at Marina Bay Sands light show.

**Day 2:** Cultural trail — Chinatown, Little India, Kampong Glam. End with hawker dinner at Lau Pa Sat.

## Three-Day Itinerary (Classic)

**Day 1 — Modern Singapore**
- Morning: Marina Bay Sands SkyPark or observation deck
- Midday: Gardens by the Bay (outdoor Supertrees and indoor domes)
- Afternoon: ArtScience Museum or shopping at Marina Bay
- Evening: Spectra light and water show at Marina Bay

**Day 2 — Heritage Trail**
- Morning: Chinatown (Buddha Tooth Relic Temple, heritage shophouses)
- Lunch: Maxwell Food Centre or Chinatown Complex
- Afternoon: Little India (Sri Veeramakaliamman Temple, Mustafa Centre)
- Evening: Kampong Glam and Sultan Mosque; dinner on Arab Street

**Day 3 — Nature and Wildlife**
- Morning: Singapore Zoo (allow 3–4 hours)
- Afternoon: River Wonders or relax at Singapore Botanic Gardens
- Evening: Optional Night Safari or Orchard Road shopping

## Family-Friendly Three-Day Plan

**Day 1:** Sentosa — S.E.A. Aquarium, beach, cable car  
**Day 2:** Singapore Zoo and KidZworld; optional Night Safari  
**Day 3:** Gardens by the Bay (Children's Garden) and Science Centre

## Cultural Immersion Itinerary

**Day 1:** National Museum and National Gallery  
**Day 2:** Chinatown Heritage Centre, Thian Hock Keng Temple, Peranakan Museum  
**Day 3:** Kampong Glam, Malay Heritage Centre, Joo Chiat shophouses

## Things to Do by Interest

### Art and Museums
- National Gallery Singapore
- ArtScience Museum
- National Museum of Singapore
- Asian Civilisations Museum

### Nature and Parks
- Singapore Botanic Gardens
- MacRitchie Reservoir and TreeTop Walk
- Southern Ridges (Henderson Waves)
- Pulau Ubin (island day trip)

### Shopping
- Orchard Road malls
- Bugis Street market
- Mustafa Centre (24-hour)
- VivoCity (Sentosa gateway)

### Nightlife and Entertainment
- Clarke Quay riverside bars
- Marina Bay Sands casino (for adults)
- Night Safari
- Spectra light show (free)

## Indoor vs Outdoor Planning

**Prefer outdoor when:** Clear skies, moderate humidity, early morning or late afternoon slots.

**Switch to indoor when:** Heavy rain forecast, afternoon thunderstorms, extreme heat.

**Indoor alternatives for popular outdoor spots:**
| Outdoor Plan | Indoor Alternative |
|--------------|-------------------|
| Supertree Grove walk | Cloud Forest dome |
| East Coast Park | S.E.A. Aquarium |
| Southern Ridges hike | National Gallery |
| Beach at Sentosa | Trick Eye Museum or indoor mall |

## Budget-Friendly Tips

- Eat at hawker centres (meals from SGD 5–10)
- Use MRT instead of taxis
- Many parks and heritage walks are free
- Free light shows at Marina Bay (Spectra)

## Suggested Daily Budget Ranges (Excluding Accommodation)

- Budget traveller: SGD 50–80 per day
- Mid-range: SGD 100–150 per day
- Comfortable: SGD 200+ per day
